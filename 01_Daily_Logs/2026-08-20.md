## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Designing a Reproducible Experimental Protocol
- **Ablation study design:** isolating exactly one variable at a time to attribute an effect correctly — the same discipline as Week 1-3's "one hyperparameter change per correction."
- **Risk/limitation identification *before* running experiments:** naming what could invalidate the results in advance (data contamination, model too small, metric doesn't capture the phenomenon) rather than discovering it only after the fact.
