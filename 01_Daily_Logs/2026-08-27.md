## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Data Documentation and Contamination Checks
- **A dataset description sheet as a research artifact, not bureaucracy:** license, source, size, and construction method written down once so no later step (training, publication) has to re-derive or guess it.
- **Manual inspection before automation:** looking at raw examples by hand catches formatting bugs, mislabeled entries, or off-target-language content that an aggregate statistic would hide entirely.
- **Contamination vs. intentional overlap:** the general ML rule ("train and test must never share content") applies to UbuntuGuard's own train/test split, but not to Native-DPO vs. Translated-DPO sharing English source text — that overlap is the whole point of the H1 comparison, and needs to be argued explicitly rather than assumed obvious.
