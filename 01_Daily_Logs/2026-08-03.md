## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Pretraining, SFT, and the Loss Function
- **Pretraining vs. Supervised Fine-Tuning:** pretraining learns general language ability from a massive, naturally-occurring corpus; SFT adapts that ability to a specific behavior (following instructions) using a much smaller, curated dataset — it teaches *behavior*, not new knowledge.
- **Task-Specific Fine-Tuning vs. Instruction Tuning:** task-specific fine-tuning specializes a model for one narrow task; instruction tuning teaches a general "follow whatever instruction you're given" behavior across many task types at once (exactly why Dolly-15k spans 8 categories).
- **The Causal Language-Modeling Loss:** the same next-token cross-entropy loss used for pretraining and for `compute_perplexity()` — SFT doesn't introduce a new loss function, it just changes *what data* that loss is computed on.
