## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Overfitting, Validation, and Why Week 1 Was "Flying Blind"
- **Overfitting:** a model's performance on training data improving while its performance on unseen (held-out) data gets worse — exactly what happened Week 1, only discovered *after* the fact because nothing was monitored during training.
- **Training and Validation Curves:** tracking both the training loss and a separate validation loss over time — divergence between the two (training loss falling, validation loss rising or flat) is the direct, real-time signal of overfitting.
- **Perplexity and Its Limitations:** a single perplexity number at the end tells you *that* something went wrong, not *when* it started going wrong — validation curves during training are what make a run correctable rather than just diagnosable after the fact.
