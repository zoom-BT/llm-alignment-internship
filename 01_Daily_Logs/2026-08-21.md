## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### From Proposal to Approved Research Plan
- **Scope discipline in a written proposal:** stating clearly what the project *will not* attempt, not just what it will — narrowing scope is itself a research skill.
- **Schedule realism:** mapping the proposal's experimental protocol onto 4 concrete weeks, informed directly by how long Week 1-3's actual experiments took (not an idealized estimate).
- **The approval gate:** why a supervisor sign-off before major experiments begin matters methodologically, not just administratively — catching a flawed research design early is far cheaper than discovering it after Week 6's main experiments.
