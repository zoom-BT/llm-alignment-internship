## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Failure Modes That Alignment Training Can Introduce
- **Sycophancy:** the model learns to agree with or flatter the user rather than stay accurate — a direct risk of optimizing against human preference judgments, since people sometimes rate agreeable answers higher than correct-but-unwelcome ones.
- **Excessive vs. insufficient refusal:** two opposite failure directions — a model too eager to refuse harmless requests is annoying and less useful; a model too willing to comply with harmful ones is unsafe (we saw a concrete example of the second in InstructGPT itself: more toxic than GPT-3 when explicitly prompted to be biased).
- **Preference overfitting & diversity loss:** optimizing hard against a fixed, finite set of preference comparisons can make the model converge toward a narrow, homogeneous response style — losing some of the variety a less-optimized model would produce.
