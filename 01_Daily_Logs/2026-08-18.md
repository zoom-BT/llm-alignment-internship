## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### From Theme to Testable Hypothesis
- **Research context vs. research question:** the context motivates *why* the question matters; the question itself must be answerable by a specific experiment, not just "interesting to think about."
- **Baseline selection:** every research question needs a defined baseline to measure against — reusing Week 1-3's evaluation patterns (a "before" condition) rather than inventing a new evaluation methodology from scratch.
- **Related work framing:** situating a research question against what's already been studied (and what specifically hasn't) — the "gap" the project would fill, even a small one.
