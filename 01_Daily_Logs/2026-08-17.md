## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Formulating a Research Question
- **Precise vs. broad research question:** the difference between "does language affect model safety" (too broad, not testable) and "does automatic translation of a safety benchmark change refusal rate on a specific model, for a specific language pair" (precise, testable, has a clear metric).
- **Falsifiability:** a research question needs an answer that a real experiment could show to be *false* — not just confirm what's already assumed.
- **Feasibility triage:** matching a research idea against actual available resources (compute, data, time) *before* getting attached to it, rather than after.
