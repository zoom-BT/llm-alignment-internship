## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Feedback Types and the Limits of Pure Demonstration Learning
- **Demonstrations vs. Preferences vs. Rankings vs. Verifiable Rewards:** the spectrum of signal a model can learn from, from "here's a good answer" (expensive to write, easy to learn from) to "here's a check that passes/fails" (cheap to compute, needs a verifiable task).
- **Why SFT alone is limited:** it only ever sees positive examples — it never learns what a *bad* response looks like, or how much worse one bad response is than another. This is exactly the gap RLHF/DPO-family methods are built to fill.
- **Rejection sampling / RAFT:** generate several candidates, keep the best (by a human, reward model, AI judge, or verifier), fine-tune on the kept ones only — a middle ground between plain SFT and full RL.
