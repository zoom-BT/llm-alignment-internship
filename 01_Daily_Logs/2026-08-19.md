## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Data and Model Selection for a New Research Question
- **License/accessibility check before commitment:** verifying a dataset's license permits the intended use *before* building an experimental pipeline around it, not after.
- **Metric validity:** choosing a metric that actually measures the phenomenon in the research question, not just a convenient number that's easy to compute (a callback to Week 2's perplexity-limitations discussion).
- **Matching model scale to the question:** whether a 0.5B-class model can meaningfully exhibit/measure the phenomenon under study, or whether the topic needs a larger (instruction-tuned, multilingual) base model.
