## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Beyond PPO: Simpler and Group-Based RL for LMs
- **REINFORCE:** the simplest policy-gradient method — no value function/critic, just the raw reward times the log-probability of the action taken, higher variance than PPO but far simpler to implement.
- **RLOO (REINFORCE Leave-One-Out):** critic-free — instead of a learned value function, it uses the *other* sampled responses to the same prompt as the baseline for computing advantage, removing the need to train a separate value model.
- **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization):** samples several responses per prompt, computes each one's advantage *relative to the group's average reward* — designed for verifiable-reward settings (math, code) where a reward model isn't even needed, just a correctness checker.
- **Evaluator limitations:** an LLM-as-judge or reward model isn't a ground truth — it can favor longer answers regardless of quality (length bias), favor whichever answer appears first/second (position bias), or be gamed by the policy it's supposed to be judging (reward hacking, already seen concretely with InstructGPT's SFT-vs-PPO hallucination result).
