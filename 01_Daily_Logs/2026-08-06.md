## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Evaluation Methods and What "Better" Actually Means
- **Automatic Evaluation:** perplexity, BLEU/ROUGE-style metrics — fast, cheap, reproducible, but blind to whether a response is actually *useful*.
- **Human Evaluation:** manually reading generated responses (today's 20-example review) — slower, but catches failure modes a single aggregate number hides, like the Week 1 repetition loop (`'2+2=4ecimal\ncimal\necimal'`).
- **Memorizing vs. Learning a Response Style vs. Acquiring a New Capability:** the three things SFT could be doing to a model, in increasing order of what we actually want — Week 1's result (great training loss, terrible test perplexity) was memorization, not style transfer or new capability.
