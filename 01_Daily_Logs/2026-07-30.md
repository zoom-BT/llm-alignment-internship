## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) & Memory Optimization
- **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** Adapting pre-trained weights on instruction-response pairs using cross-entropy loss.
- **Gradient Accumulation:** Calculating gradients over multiple small mini-batches before executing an optimizer step to simulate larger batch sizes on limited VRAM.
- **Gradient Checkpointing:** Trading computation for memory by recomputing intermediate activations during the backward pass instead of storing them.
