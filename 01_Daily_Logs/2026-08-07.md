## Concepts Covered Today (In English)
### Data Quality, Contamination, and Closing the Loop
- **Data Quality and Diversity:** not just "more data," but data that's representative, non-redundant, and free of near-duplicates — directly informed by Week 1's discovery of a duplicated instruction across train/test.
- **Data Deduplication:** the concrete fix for that discovery — removing or isolating near-identical examples before splitting, so train/test contamination isn't left to chance.
- **Training and Evaluation Contamination:** the broader principle behind both of the above — any leakage between what a model is trained on and what it's judged on inflates apparent performance without reflecting real capability.
